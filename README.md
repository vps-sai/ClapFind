# ClapFind

Clap three times or whistle, and your Android phone rings, vibrates and flashes its torch.
Free, no ads, no data collection. The app has no internet permission.

## Get the installable APK (no tools needed on your computer)

1. Put these files in a GitHub repository (branch `main`).
2. Open the repository's **Actions** tab. A "Build APK" run starts by itself and takes about 3 minutes.
3. Open the finished run and download **ClapFind-apk** at the bottom of the page.
4. Unzip it, copy `app-debug.apk` to the phone, tap it and allow installation.

## Using it

Open ClapFind, tap **Turn on**, allow the microphone and notifications. Use **Test ring** to check the alarm,
then put the phone down, walk away and clap three times (about three claps a second) or whistle one steady note for a second.
Unlocking the phone, or tapping the notification, stops the ringing. It also stops by itself after 45 seconds.

After restarting the phone, open the app once (or tap its reminder) to switch listening back on. Android does not allow
an app to switch the microphone on by itself.
