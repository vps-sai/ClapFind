# ClapFind privacy policy

ClapFind collects no data.

- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
- The microphone is used only to detect claps and whistles. Sound is analysed in memory for loudness and pitch, then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or shared.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no location.
- The only things stored on the phone are your settings (on/off, clap, whistle, Wi-Fi only, sensitivity).

Permissions used: microphone (to hear the clap or whistle), notifications (to show that it is listening and the "stop ringing" button), network state (only to know whether the phone is on Wi-Fi), vibration, run at startup (to remind you to switch listening back on after a restart).
