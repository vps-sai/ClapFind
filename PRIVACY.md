# ClapFind privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

ClapFind is a free Android app that rings your phone when it hears three claps or a whistle. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary

ClapFind collects no data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## Microphone

ClapFind uses the microphone, including while the app is closed or the screen is off, for one purpose: to detect claps and whistles so that it can ring the phone. Sound is analysed on the phone, in memory, for loudness and pitch only, and is discarded immediately. Audio is never recorded, saved, transmitted or shared. While ClapFind is listening, Android shows a notification and the microphone indicator. You can turn listening off at any time in the app or from the notification.

## What the app does not do

- No personal data is collected, stored or shared.
- No internet access, sign-in, accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- No location, contacts, photos or files are accessed.

## What is stored on your phone

Only your settings: whether listening is on, clap and whistle options, the Wi-Fi-only option, sensitivity and the chosen alarm sound. These stay on the phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- Microphone: to hear the clap or whistle.
- Notifications: to show that the app is listening and to offer the "Stop ringing" button.
- Network state: only to know whether the phone is connected to Wi-Fi. No network traffic is sent.
- Vibration, and run at startup (to remind you to switch listening back on after a restart).

## Children

ClapFind is a general utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.
